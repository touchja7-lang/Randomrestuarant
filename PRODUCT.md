# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Hungry individuals, couples, and coworker groups facing mealtime indecision ("วันนี้กินอะไรดี?"). They want an immediate, confident food recommendation without wading through cluttered review feeds or endless chat debates.

## Product Purpose
Eliminate food decision paralysis through a fast, delightful restaurant randomizer with smart filtering (tags, budget, open status) and direct Google Maps access.

## Brand Personality
Appetizing, Decisive, Crisp.
- Voice: energetic, friendly, punchy, culinary-conscious.
- Emotional goal: relief from decision fatigue, appetite spark, spontaneous fun.

## Anti-references
- Drab enterprise gray dashboards and sterile corporate templates.
- 2026 AI slop tropes: cream/beige paper backgrounds, repetitive uppercase tracked eyebrows, decorative gradient text.
- Over-the-top casino slots with sluggish 10-second animations that frustrate hungry users.
- Nested card labyrinths and low-contrast muted typography.

## Design Principles
1. **Appetite First**: Rich culinary palette (crimson-vermilion primary, warm saffron accent) anchored on pure white canvas with strong contrast.
2. **Instant Gratification**: Zero friction to spin; clear tactile filter toggles that recalculate options effortlessly.
3. **Tactile Decisiveness**: A crisp, responsive roulette ticker transition that feels exciting yet snappy (under 1.5s).
4. **Earned Familiarity**: Linear/Stripe-level craft in inputs, buttons, status badges, empty states, and responsive layout across all pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High text-to-background contrast (≥ 7:1 for body copy against pure canvas).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with instantaneous pick reveals.
- Visible, accessible focus rings on all interactive chips, inputs, and triggers.
- Fluid Thai and Latin typographic pairings with generous line-heights.
